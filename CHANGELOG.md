## [Unreleased]

### Added

- First cut: a bonsai in a pot on the desktop, grown from the Greenlight running on the
  machine. A leaf for every passing build, and a new branch once the pads already out are
  mostly full.
- A break turns a share of the leaves red and drops them; a pipeline left broken wilts the
  tree yellow, then brown, and keeps shedding until it is fixed. Fixed, it heals over a couple
  of hours.
- A caterpillar for each pull request waiting on you, fatter the longer it waits, leaving as a
  butterfly once reviewed.
- A stronger breeze and a glowing bud while a build runs; a dormant grey tree when Greenlight
  is away.
- The tree is saved to `%AppData%\Greenlight.Bonsai\tree.json`, so it survives restarts.
